# 05 — Naming

Criteria: says "brain + rank" in one word, ≤10 letters, pronounceable in English, not an existing Play title, no visible trademark conflict, fits a 30-character Play title alongside keywords.

| Candidate | Play Store exact-title hit | Web / trademark check | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neurank | none | "NeuroRank" AI product (2026) + active US mark "NEURAL RANK" | ✗ confusion risk |
| MindRank | "MindRank: Quiz Battle RPG" | — | ✗ taken |
| Brainrank | "Brainrank: Reflex & Brain Test" | — | ✗ taken |
| Cognara | "Cognara: Brain Training Games" | — | ✗ taken |
| Synaptiq / Synaply | several | — | ✗ taken |
| Mindladder | none | none found; but "LADDER" is the #1 fitness app brand | ~ avoid association |
| Thinkrank / Witrank / Cerebrank | none | none found | ok, less evocative |
| Mindclimb / Brainclimb | none | "Brain Climb" iOS learning app exists | ~ |
| **Mindtier** | **none** | **no app, company or trademark found** | **✓ chosen** |

**Mindtier**: "mind" + "tier" (the rank tiers: Iron → Grandmaster). It reads instantly as "what tier is your mind?".

- Play title (30 max): `Mindtier: Ranked Brain Games` (28 chars).
- Short description (80 max): `Find your brain rank. Climb tiers in daily brain games. No ads, ever.`
- Android package: `app.mindtier`. iOS bundle: `app.mindtier`.

Remaining legal step (manual, see HANDOFF): run a USPTO / EUIPO / WIPO search for "MINDTIER" in classes 9, 41 and 42 before spending on marketing. Web and store checks are clean, but they are not a legal clearance.
